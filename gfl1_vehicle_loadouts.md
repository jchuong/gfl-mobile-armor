# GFL1 Mobile Vehicle Loadouts

Synthesized from the gfverse Mobile Armor guide \+ varz's Component Tierlist. Talents matter far more than rarity — every slot below lists the ideal talent to hunt for, but a good component with *any* listed talent beats a perfect one with none.

**Slot recap**

- **Tank:** Flex weapon \+ Heavy weapon \+ Light weapon \+ Defensive \+ Function \+ 4 chips  
- **Mech:** Flex weapon \+ 2 Light weapons \+ Defensive \+ Function \+ 4 chips (plus melee mode)  
- Reload is dead on Heavy weapons (only the Grenade Launcher uses it at all) — stack Damage instead.  
- Manning bonus scales with *level only*, not the unit's stats. Fill every slot with type-appropriate units at cap (90+ Commander/Driver, \~80 Gunner/Loader).

---

## 1\. General-Purpose Tank

The all-rounder. Two Heavies for remote bombardment, one Light to mop up and chip bosses.

| Slot | Component | Talent to hunt |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Flex (Heavy) | **Cannon** — best AoE \+ knockback | Heavy Weapon Shield / Weak Point Targeting |
| Heavy | **Grenade Launcher** — single-target/tiny AoE | Heavy Weapon Shield (pairs great with GL) |
| Light | **Lightweight Rapid-Fire Railgun** (mount in the slot *furthest* from enemies) | Mobbed Damage Reduction / Melee Attack |
| Defensive | **Shield Generator** or **Automated Defense System** | Shield Deflection (−20% dmg) |
| Function | **Signal Receiver** / **Laser Rangefinder** / **Jammer** | Charge Conversion (+\~33% Armor) or Debuff Amplification |

**Chips (aim \~40 total Armor):** 2× Armor (Armor/Eva) \+ Firepower (Heavy/Light Dmg) \+ Light Damage (Light Dmg/RoF). Armor talent chips are effectively mandatory; after armor, prioritize Light Weapon \> Firepower \> RoF \> Crit.

---

## 2\. General-Purpose Mech

Tankier, thrives up close, has a melee mode. One Heavy in the Flex, two Lights.

| Slot | Component | Talent to hunt |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Flex (Heavy) | **Cannon** (AoE/KB) or **Grenade Launcher** (single-target) | Heavy Weapon Shield / Weak Point Targeting |
| Light | **Lightweight Rapid-Fire Railgun** | Mobbed Damage Reduction |
| Light | **Rotating Turret** (backline killer) or **Anti-Armor Sniper Rifle** (single-target/backline) | Melee Attack / Debuff Amplification |
| Defensive | **Shield Generator** or **Automated Defense System** | Shield Deflection |
| Function | **Signal Receiver** / **Jammer** | Charge Conversion (+Armor) |

**Chips:** same principle — hit the Armor threshold first, then Firepower/Light Damage. The Mech's survivability lets you lean slightly more offensive once armor is met.

## 3\. ⭐ Floor 35 — Tank

**Enemies:** Paradeus (Slow Shock) \+ **Nemhran**. No Ladons, thankfully. The whole fight is: survive Paradeus, break Nemhran's constant HP shields, don't get orbed to death.

| Slot | Component | Why |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Flex (Heavy) | **Cannon** | Knockback to reposition Paradeus \+ move out of Nemhran's orb pools |
| Heavy | **Grenade Launcher** — talent: **Heavy Weapon Shield** | Survive orb attacks |
| Light | **Lightweight Rapid-Fire Railgun** ← *mandatory* | Ignores HP shields every 2nd attack — your reliable answer to Nemhran's self-shielding |
| Defensive | **Shield Generator** — talent: **Shield Deflection** | Max damage mitigation |
| Function | **Charge Conversion** (Armor) or **Smoke Grenade Launcher** vs boss | Push armor as high as possible |

**Chips:** stack Armor hard here — bring as much armor \+ shielding as the build allows, more than your general setup.

**Nemhran options (pick one):**

1. Grab the **"wipe enemy shields"** buff from the Gray Zone nodes (carries into the Tower).  
2. Let the **Railgun** do the shield-breaking passively.  
3. **Fight her with the NPC echelon** — *and have the Tank provide HOC support*, or you'll fail to break her shield at low HP (Suomi's damage scales off her current HP). Reset until SP9 or Suomi is targeted when Nemhran flies; Suomi can facetank, SP9 dodges. Ribeyrolles' skill is tilescan, so keep the other ARs above/below her.

Path through helipads to repair/resupply — energy management matters on this floor.

## 4\. ⭐ Floor 35 — Mech

Same enemies, but the Mech has a trump card: **melee mode.** At Tech Tree level 3, the sword's bonus damage ignores HP shields, so you can just get in Nemhran's face and bonk her.

| Slot | Component | Why |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Flex (Heavy) | **Cannon** | Knockback for Paradeus / spacing |
| Light | **Lightweight Rapid-Fire Railgun** | Shield-ignore backup for when you're not in melee range |
| Light | **Anti-Armor Sniper Rifle** or **Rotating Turret** | Backline / single-target pressure |
| Defensive | **Shield Generator** — talent: **Shield Deflection** | Mitigation |
| Function | **Charge Conversion** (Armor) | Max armor |

**Plan:** clear Paradeus with Cannon \+ Lights, then switch to melee mode and sword Nemhran down (needs Tech Tree lv3). Keep the Railgun as insurance if you get knocked out of melee. Max armor \+ shielding on chips.

## Farming note (floors 31–34, on the way to 35\)

If you're sweeping 31–35 for drops (Heavy weapons \+ chips for Tank, Light weapons \+ chips for Mech), the mid-floors want a slightly different light weapon:

- **Floors 32/34:** swap in a **Heavy Machine Gun** (⚠️ set to **forced manual**) or keep the **Railgun** for piercing armored SWAP SF, plus **Cannon** for knockbacks.  
- **Floor 34 (Hydras):** Cannon knockback is key; **Jammer Gun**, **Pre-Emptive Suppression**, or **Stun Attack** help lock them down. Vehicles have no i-frames — Hydras can one-shot, so reset if they get close. (CN already nerfed their move speed 20→8; coming to EN.)  
- **Floor 31 (Scarecrow):** do **not** fight Scarecrow with the vehicle (no i-frames) — let the NPC team handle her.
